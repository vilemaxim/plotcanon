## Summary

A harbour pilot who knows every channel through the reef.

## Background

Lost a boat on the reef as a girl and has not lost one since.

## Portrayal

Forties, brisk, laughs at her own jokes. Player-facing guide.

## Personality & Goals

Wants to be paid and to be home before dark.

## Knowledge & Secrets

Knows the ferryman has started crossing at night with no lamp.

## Relationships

Cousin to half the harbour. Dislikes the smuggler on principle.

## Current Situation

Working the harbour mouth every tide.

## GM Notes

Safe to use anywhere. Nothing depends on her.
