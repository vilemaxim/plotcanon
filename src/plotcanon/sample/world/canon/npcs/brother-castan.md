## Summary

The younger keeper of the chapel. Wants to open the crypt.

## Background

A foundling the order took in. The chapel is the only home he has had.

## Portrayal

Thirties, earnest, impatient. Can fight if cornered.

## Personality & Goals

Wants to know what the order has been guarding, and is done waiting to be told.

## Knowledge & Secrets

Has been diving to the crypt door at the lowest tides. It is not locked from this side.

## Relationships

Loves the sister and no longer obeys her.

## Current Situation

Has rope and lamps hidden on the reef for the storm moon.

## GM Notes

He opens the crypt if nobody stops him. That is the hook for the vigil mod.
