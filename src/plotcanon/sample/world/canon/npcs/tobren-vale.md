## Summary

Master of the salt families. Proud, tired and cornered.

## Background

Elected guildmaster twenty years ago after his brother drowned.

## Portrayal

Sixties, heavy, a voice that carries down the stair. A talker who can turn a crowd.

## Personality & Goals

Wants the tax halved and his daughter kept out of it.

## Knowledge & Secrets

Knows the Guild cannot pay even the old tax this year. The pans are failing.

## Relationships

Blames the Wardens' captain for his brother's death. He is wrong, and half knows it.

## Current Situation

Holding the stair with fifty pan-workers and no plan for what happens when the picket moves.

## GM Notes

He will not start the fight. He will not stop one either.
