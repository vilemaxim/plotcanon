## Summary

Runs untaxed salt out past the light. Friend to nobody and useful to everyone.

## Background

A pan-worker's son who bought a boat and never looked back.

## Portrayal

Forties, easy smile, hands never still. Needs someone who can lose a fight convincingly.

## Personality & Goals

Wants the tax to stay doubled. Nobody buys smuggled salt when the legal kind is cheap.

## Knowledge & Secrets

Is hiding the Guild's clerk, and has read the ledger.

## Relationships

Sells to the Guild, bribes one Warden sergeant, and is owed money by the ferryman.

## Current Situation

Visible in the harbour by day, as if he had nothing to hide.

## GM Notes

Three mods across both campaigns need him alive. Check all of them before anyone kills him.
