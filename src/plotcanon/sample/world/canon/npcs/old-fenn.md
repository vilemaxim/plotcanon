## Summary

Keeper of the light for forty years. Let it go dark once this spring and will not say why.

## Background

Took the post the year before the chapel sank. Watched it go.

## Portrayal

Eighties, deaf in one ear, sharper than he lets on. A talker; frail.

## Personality & Goals

Wants to die before the bell rings again.

## Knowledge & Secrets

Saw something walk out of the sea the night the light went dark, and put the lamp out so it could not find the shore.

## Relationships

Feeds the clerk who is hiding in his boat shed and asks no questions.

## Current Situation

Keeping the lamp lit and the shutters on the seaward side closed.

## GM Notes

Lanternlight's keeper mod is built on what he saw. If he dies first it has no witness.
