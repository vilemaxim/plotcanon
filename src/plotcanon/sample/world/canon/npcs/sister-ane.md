## Summary

Elder of the two who keep vigil over the sunken chapel.

## Background

Was a novice the night the sea took it. Has kept every storm-moon vigil since.

## Portrayal

Seventies, straight-backed, unhurried. A talker; no combat.

## Personality & Goals

Wants the bell to stay silent one more year.

## Knowledge & Secrets

Heard the bell ring this spring, on the night the light went dark. Has told only the brother.

## Relationships

Raised the brother from a boy. Distrusts the lighthouse keeper for reasons she will not give.

## Current Situation

Preparing the storm-moon vigil and afraid of it for the first time.

## GM Notes

She knows what the tidemother is. Do not let her say it before the bell mod.
