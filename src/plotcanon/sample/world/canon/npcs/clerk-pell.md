## Summary

Keeper of the Guild's ledger. Vanished with it at the spring ebb.

## Background

Thirty years the Guild's clerk. Never missed a day until this April.

## Portrayal

Small, precise, ink on his cuffs. A nervous talker; no combat.

## Personality & Goals

Wants the ledger read by someone who will act on it, and does not know who that is.

## Knowledge & Secrets

The ledger shows the Guild has been underpaying for six years, and who in the Wardens was paid to look away.

## Relationships

Taught the guildmaster's daughter to read. Owes the smuggler a favour he regrets.

## Current Situation

Gone since the Sunday of the spring ebb. Last seen walking toward the mud at dusk with the ledger under his coat.

## GM Notes

He is alive and hiding in the boat shed at the light. The smuggler put him there.
