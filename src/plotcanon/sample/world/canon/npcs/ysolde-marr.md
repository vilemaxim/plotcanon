## Summary

Captain of the Wardens. Collecting a tax she thinks is a mistake.

## Background

Born on this coast, trained in the capital, sent back because nobody else wanted the posting.

## Portrayal

Fifties, weathered, never raises her voice. Needs someone who can command a room by standing still.

## Personality & Goals

Wants the season to end with nobody dead. Will enforce the law anyway.

## Knowledge & Secrets

Has written twice to the capital asking for the tax to be halved. Has told nobody.

## Relationships

Was once to marry the guildmaster's brother. Neither family speaks of it.

## Current Situation

Sleeping at the customs house and walking the picket herself.

## GM Notes

If she dies the Wardens have no one who wants peace.
