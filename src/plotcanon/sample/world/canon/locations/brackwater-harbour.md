## Summary

A tidal harbour that empties to mud twice a day. Everything on this coast passes through it.

## Description

Two stone moles, a customs house and a market that sets up on the mud at low water.

## Who Holds It

The Wardens, from the customs house.

## Current Situation

Busy and tense. Tax notices on every door.

## GM Notes

The market on the mud is where anyone can be found by anyone.
