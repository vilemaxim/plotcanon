## Summary

Three hundred steps cut into the cliff from the town down to the salt pans.

## Description

Wide enough for two with yokes. No rail. A landing every fifty steps with a Guild lamp.

## Who Holds It

The Guild by ancient right. The Wardens have stood a picket on the top landing since the tax doubled.

## Current Situation

Both sides are on it every night and neither will step back first.

## GM Notes

Any fight here ends with someone going over the edge. Decide who before the mod runs.
