## Summary

A chapel on the reef that the sea took thirty years ago. Its roof shows at the lowest tides.

## Description

Slate roof, a stump of bell tower, the nave full of sand. The crypt door is below the waterline.

## Who Holds It

The two who are left of its order, as far as anyone can hold a ruin.

## Current Situation

Visited at every storm moon by its keepers and avoided by everyone else.

## GM Notes

The bell is still hung. It should not be able to ring under water.
