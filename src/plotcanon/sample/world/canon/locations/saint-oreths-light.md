## Summary

The lighthouse at the harbour mouth, a mile out along the reef.

## Description

A white tower, a keeper's cottage and a boat shed. Reached on foot only at the bottom of the ebb.

## Who Holds It

Nobody. The keeper is paid by the crown and takes orders from no one.

## Current Situation

Lit every night but one this spring. Nobody has explained the one.

## GM Notes

From the lamp room you can see the sunken chapel's roof at low water. The keeper does not look.
