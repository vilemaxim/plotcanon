## Premise

The crown doubled the salt tax. The Guild will not pay it and the Wardens must collect it.

## Current State

A standoff on the stair. The ledger that would settle what is owed is missing with its clerk.

## Where It Could Go

- Whoever reads the ledger first decides who is ruined by it.
- The captain's letters to the capital could be answered.
- The smuggler profits from every week it lasts.

## GM Notes

It should end at Storm Moon one way or another. Lanternlight then lives with the result.
