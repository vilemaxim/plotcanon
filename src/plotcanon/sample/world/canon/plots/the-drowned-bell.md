## Premise

A bell under the sea rang this spring, and the light went dark the same night.

## Current State

The order's younger keeper means to open the crypt at the storm moon.

## Where It Could Go

- The crypt opens and something answers.
- The keeper finally says what he saw.
- The bell rings again and the whole harbour hears it.

## GM Notes

Runs across both campaigns. Tideward opens the crypt; Lanternlight goes down into it.
