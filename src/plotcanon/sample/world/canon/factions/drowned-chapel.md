## Summary

An order of two that keeps vigil over a chapel the sea took a generation ago.

## Goals

Keep the bell under the water silent.

## Structure & Notable Members

One sister and one brother. There were eleven when the chapel stood.

## Standing

Thought harmless and a little sad. Given bread by the Guild and left alone by the Wardens.

## Current Situation

Holding a vigil at every storm moon, as they have since the chapel sank.

## GM Notes

They are right about the bell. They are wrong about what rings it.
