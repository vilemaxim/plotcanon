## Summary

The families who work the salt pans and own the stair down to them.

## Goals

Have the tax halved again, by petition if possible and by not paying if not.

## Structure & Notable Members

A guildmaster elected for life, a clerk who keeps the ledger, and nine pan-holding families.

## Standing

Feeds the town and employs a third of it. Cannot be arrested without the harbour stopping.

## Current Situation

Withholding the tax. The ledger that proves how much is owed has gone missing with its clerk.

## GM Notes

The Guild did not hide the ledger. It would very much like to know who did.
