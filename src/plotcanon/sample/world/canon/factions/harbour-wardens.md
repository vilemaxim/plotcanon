## Summary

The crown's law on the Brackwater coast: forty men, two cutters and a tax to collect.

## Goals

Collect the doubled salt tax without starting a war over it.

## Structure & Notable Members

A captain, four sergeants and the rank and file. The captain answers to nobody nearer than the capital.

## Standing

Obeyed in the harbour, resented on the stair, ignored past the light.

## Current Situation

Stretched thin. Half the company is posted on the stair every night.

## GM Notes

The Wardens are not corrupt. They are outnumbered and they know it.
