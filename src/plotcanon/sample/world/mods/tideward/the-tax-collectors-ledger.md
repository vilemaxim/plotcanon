## Premise

The Guild's ledger and its clerk are both missing. Whoever finds either first holds the whole dispute.

## Hooks

- The guildmaster asks quietly for help.
- A Warden is told to find the ledger before the Guild does.
- The smuggler lets it be known he might know something.

## Scene Breakdown

### 1. The empty counting room

**Find:** a desk cleared in a hurry. **If players do nothing:** the Guild locks the room.

### 2. What the smuggler wants

**Find:** a price. **If players do nothing:** he sells to the other side.

### 3. The clerk

**Find:** a frightened man with a book. **If players do nothing:** he stays hidden another month.

## NPC Casting

The clerk is a nervous talker. The smuggler must enjoy bargaining.

## Player Agency Notes

Players can fail, there is more than one way in, and the ending turns on what they choose.

## Possible Outcomes

The ledger goes to the Guild, to the Wardens, or into the sea.

## Rewards

The ledger itself.

## Logistics

Three small rooms, eight players, 90 minutes. Scene 3 was not reached at Low Tide.

## GM Notes

Interrupted on the Sunday of Low Tide after scene 1. Scenes 2 and 3 are still to play at Storm Moon.
