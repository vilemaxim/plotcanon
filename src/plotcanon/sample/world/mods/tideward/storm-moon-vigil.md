## Premise

The order keeps its vigil on the reef. The young brother means to open the crypt before dawn.

## Hooks

- The sister asks for witnesses.
- The brother asks for hands on the rope.
- A diver knows the crypt door.

## Scene Breakdown

### 1. The vigil

**Find:** two keepers who are not speaking. **If players do nothing:** the brother slips away at the third hour.

### 2. The crypt door

**Find:** a door that opens outward. **If players do nothing:** it opens anyway.

### 3. The turn of the tide

**Find:** a figure on the reef. **If players do nothing:** she watches until the water covers the door.

## NPC Casting

The sister needs gravity. The brother needs urgency. The woman on the reef does not speak.

## Player Agency Notes

Players can fail, there is more than one way in, and the ending turns on what they choose.

## Possible Outcomes

The crypt stays shut, opens and is closed again, or stays open.

## Rewards

What is behind the door.

## Logistics

Outdoor at night, twelve players, 90 minutes. Needs the lowest tide.

## GM Notes

Whatever happens to the crypt sets up the Lanternlight bell mod.
