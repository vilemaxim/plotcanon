## Premise

The light did not show last night. A boat goes out along the reef at the ebb to ask why.

## Hooks

- A fishing family lost nets on the reef in the dark.
- The physician is asked to look in on the keeper.

## Scene Breakdown

### 1. The walk out

**Find:** the reef bare and the tower shuttered. **If players do nothing:** the tide turns them back.

### 2. The keeper

**Find:** an old man who will talk about anything else. **If players do nothing:** he lights the lamp at dusk and says nothing.

## NPC Casting

The keeper must be able to deflect for twenty minutes.

## Player Agency Notes

Players can fail, there is more than one way in, and the ending turns on what they choose.

## Possible Outcomes

They learn he put it out on purpose, or they do not.

## Rewards

The keeper's trust, which Lanternlight will need.

## Logistics

Outdoor walk, six players, 60 minutes.

## GM Notes

He will not say what he saw. That is saved for the Lanternlight table.
