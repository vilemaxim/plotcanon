## Premise

The tax notice is read aloud at the market on the mud, and the crowd has to decide what to do about it.

## Hooks

- Everyone is at the market.
- Wardens are ordered to keep the peace.
- Guild players are told not to pay.

## Scene Breakdown

### 1. The notice

**Find:** the captain reading the notice. **If players do nothing:** it is read and resented.

### 2. The first refusal

**Find:** the guildmaster turning his back. **If players do nothing:** the Guild walks out.

## NPC Casting

The captain needs presence. The guildmaster needs a voice.

## Player Agency Notes

Players can fail, there is more than one way in, and the ending turns on what they choose.

## Possible Outcomes

Peaceful refusal, a scuffle, or a negotiated week's grace.

## Rewards

A week's grace if anyone asks for it well.

## Logistics

Main hall, forty players, 90 minutes.

## GM Notes

Opening set piece. Establishes both leaders in public.
