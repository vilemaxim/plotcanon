## Premise

The party needs to cross the harbour mouth, and the ferryman wants something other than copper.

## Hooks

- The chart-maker needs to reach the far mole.
- The pilot warns them off the ferry.

## Scene Breakdown

### 1. The price

**Find:** a man who will not say what he wants until they are on the water. **If players do nothing:** they walk round, half a day.

### 2. The crossing

**Find:** an unlit boat and a second passenger. **If players do nothing:** they arrive and learn nothing.

## NPC Casting

The ferryman is nearly silent. The pilot talks enough for both.

## Player Agency Notes

Players can fail, there is more than one way in, and the ending turns on what they choose.

## Possible Outcomes

They pay his price, refuse it, or learn whose debt it is.

## Rewards

Passage, and a favour owed.

## Logistics

At the table, one night.

## GM Notes

Introduces the harbour. Nothing here changed canon that matters.
