## Premise

A Warden sergeant finally serves the warrant he has been carrying, and asks the party to help bring the smuggler in.

## Hooks

- The sergeant hires them.
- The smuggler hires them first.
- The captain wants to know why the warrant took a week.

## Scene Breakdown

### 1. The warrant

**Find:** a sergeant in a hurry. **If players do nothing:** he goes alone.

### 2. The boat

**Find:** the smuggler loading at night. **If players do nothing:** he sails.

### 3. The customs house

**Find:** the captain, asking the question nobody wants asked. **If players do nothing:** the sergeant lies.

## NPC Casting

The sergeant must be able to lie badly. The smuggler must be able to run.

## Player Agency Notes

Players can fail, there is more than one way in, and the ending turns on what they choose.

## Possible Outcomes

The smuggler is taken, escapes, or talks.

## Rewards

The truth about who was paid.

## Logistics

At the table, one night.

## GM Notes

Pointless if the smuggler is already dead or gone when it runs.
