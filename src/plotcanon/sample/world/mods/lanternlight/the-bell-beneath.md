## Premise

The party goes down into the crypt under the chapel to find what rings the bell.

## Hooks

- The sister asks them to close what was opened.
- The diver has been to the door before.

## Scene Breakdown

### 1. The descent

**Find:** a stair full of sand and a rope someone left. **If players do nothing:** the tide gives them an hour.

### 2. The bell

**Find:** it, still hung, still dry. **If players do nothing:** it rings.

### 3. Who rings it

**Find:** the woman on the reef, inside. **If players do nothing:** she finishes what she came for.

## NPC Casting

The sister stays above and is heard, not seen.

## Player Agency Notes

Players can fail, there is more than one way in, and the ending turns on what they choose.

## Possible Outcomes

The bell is silenced, rung, or taken.

## Rewards

The answer to the whole plot.

## Logistics

At the table, two nights.

## GM Notes

Assumes the crypt was opened at Storm Moon and the sister survived it.
