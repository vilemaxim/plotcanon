## Premise

The party goes down into the crypt under the chapel to find what rings the bell.

## Hooks

- The sister asks them to close what was opened → [[The descent]]
- The diver has been to the door before, and knows a way past the sand → [[The bell]]

## Scene Breakdown

Written for the Dry Run convention: each scene says where it leads.

### 1. The descent

**Find:** a stair full of sand and a rope someone left.
**If players do nothing:** the tide gives them an hour, then the steps flood → [[The crypt floods]]
**Leads to:** down the rope → [[The bell]]

### 2. The bell

**Find:** it, still hung, still dry.
**If players do nothing:** it rings → [[Who rings it]]
**Leads to:** cut it down → [[The bell is taken]]; follow the sound inward → [[Who rings it]]

### 3. Who rings it

**Find:** the woman on the reef, inside.
**Contest:** stop her before the last stroke, win → [[The bell is silenced]], lose → [[The bell is rung]]
**If players do nothing:** she finishes what she came for → [[The bell is rung]]

## NPC Casting

The sister stays above and is heard, not seen.

## Player Agency Notes

Players can fail, there is more than one way in, and the ending turns on what they choose.

## Possible Outcomes

### The bell is silenced

The crypt goes quiet, and the chapel above stops hearing it.

### The bell is rung

The last stroke sounds across the water, and something answers.

### The bell is taken

The party carries it up, still dry, and now someone has to keep it.

### The crypt floods

The tide closes the way down. Nobody learns who rings the bell this time.

## Rewards

The answer to the whole plot.

## Logistics

At the table, two nights.

## GM Notes

Assumes the crypt was opened at Storm Moon and the sister survived it.
