## Premise

The lighthouse keeper is ready to say what walked out of the sea the night he put the lamp out.

## Hooks

- The physician is called back to the light.
- The diver wants to know what he saw from above.

## Scene Breakdown

### 1. The lamp room

**Find:** shutters closed on the seaward side. **If players do nothing:** he keeps them closed.

### 2. The telling

**Find:** an old man deciding whether to speak. **If players do nothing:** he does, to nobody.

### 3. The reef at the turn

**Find:** the woman on the reef, looking at the tower. **If players do nothing:** the lamp goes out again.

## NPC Casting

The keeper carries the whole mod. Frail, deaf in one ear, sharp.

## Player Agency Notes

Players can fail, there is more than one way in, and the ending turns on what they choose.

## Possible Outcomes

He tells it, takes it to his grave, or shows them instead.

## Rewards

The only eyewitness account there is.

## Logistics

At the table, one night, may run to two.

## GM Notes

Needs the keeper alive and the light standing.
