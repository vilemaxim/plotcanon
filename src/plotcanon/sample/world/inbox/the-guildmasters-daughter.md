## Premise

The guildmaster's daughter asks the captain what happened the night her uncle drowned.

## Hooks

- She asks a player to carry the message.
- The captain asks a player to be in the room.

## Scene Breakdown

### 1. The message

**Find:** a letter nobody wants to deliver. **If players do nothing:** it is not delivered.

### 2. The answer

**Find:** two people and twenty years. **If players do nothing:** the captain tells her the truth.

## NPC Casting

Both need to play a long quiet scene.

## Player Agency Notes

Players can fail, there is more than one way in, and the ending turns on what they choose.

## Possible Outcomes

The feud ends, deepens, or goes on unspoken.

## Rewards

An end to the oldest grudge on the coast.

## Logistics

One room, four players, 45 minutes.

## GM Notes

Still a draft. Needs both the captain and the guildmaster alive.
