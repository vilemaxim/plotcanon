## Summary

A disgraced knight buying his way back into a family that no longer wants him.

## Background

Second son of the Ashcrofts of Greymoor. Stripped of his commission after the
retreat at Kell's Ford — a retreat he ordered and was right to order.

## Portrayal

Mid-forties, grey at the temples, immaculate in a way that costs him effort.
Speaks quietly and makes people lean in. Needs a competent fighter.

## Personality & Goals

Wants his name back and has stopped caring what it costs.

## Knowledge & Secrets

Knows the chapel's lower door was never sealed.

## Relationships

Owes an answer to a young council clerk about Kell's Ford, and has avoided
giving it for two years.

## Current Situation

Fled north after the chapel. Whereabouts unknown to anyone in Greymoor,
including the Order, which is now hunting him too.

## GM Notes

His eventual betrayal is not for money.
