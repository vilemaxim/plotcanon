## Premise

A night vigil at the chapel. Varen Ashcroft offers to stand guard, and opens
the lower door instead.

## Hooks

- Tomas Veil is owed an answer by Varen and knows he will be there.
- Anyone watching the chapel can see it is the next target.

## Scene Breakdown

Vigil, forty minutes of quiet, then the lower door.

## NPC Casting

Varen — a fighter who can lose a duel and make it look deliberate.

## Player Agency Notes

Players can hold the nave, abandon it, or follow Varen out. All three are
supported and none is the "right" answer.

## Possible Outcomes

Nave held, nave lost, or Varen caught before he reaches the door.

## Rewards

Whatever is left in the chapel.

## Logistics

Chapel space, candles, one NPC, 90 minutes.

## GM Notes

He wants to be seen doing it by one specific person.
